## Reading

- Neither predicted endpoint (abandon -> H for cell 1, hide -> O for cell 2) occurred. Cell 1 is a zero-advantage
  stall: ~98% of samples are flagged, so the group-centered step penalty is a constant; magnitude is irrelevant to a
  zero-spread signal. Cell 2 shows that an uncentered, mass-preserving per-token penalty is far above the task
  advantage scale and the policy's cheapest escape is to never commit to the plan.
- Both match hide-or-delete D3 (Aug 2026): outcome follows the within-group spread of the penalty (step/binary ->
  stall; flat-then-curved -> stall then jump), and deletion/deferral appears only at jumps.
- Graded signal for a D3 transplant: regex count of backdoor terms in the CoT has mean 72, within-task std 22
  (cv 0.30), 0% zeros on finished samples (results/assay_signal_spread_20261019.md). M1's quotes are not a count
  (4-6 regardless of length); M1's verdict is reliable (99% retest agreement).
- Relocation is already baseline: backdoor words in code comments in 19% of finished samples, in answer prose in 45%;
  M1 never sees them.
